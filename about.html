My Story

Why I Do This Work

I created this space for women — both Muslim and non-Muslim — who want better health without compromising their values. So many women feel stuck between extreme diet culture and genuine confusion about what healthy actually looks like.

Add PCOS, hormonal imbalance, or an inconsistent routine — and it becomes completely overwhelming. I've seen it firsthand, in classrooms, in conversations, and in the kind of questions women search for at 2am.

"Wellness should feel aligned with your faith, realistic for your lifestyle, and rooted in science — not fear."

That's why I focus on practical nutrition, sustainable weight management, and balanced fitness guidance designed specifically with Muslim women in mind — though the science behind it applies to every woman who's tired of the noise.

No crash diets. No unrealistic expectations. Just steady, informed progress that actually fits your life.
